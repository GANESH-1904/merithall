Our story

MeritHall began in 2018 with two tutors and twelve students. Parents told us they wanted clear updates, not just homework help. So we built small batches and a dashboard that shows attendance, scores and fees in one place.

  • Mission: make every child a confident learner
  • Vision: a tutor-led centre families trust for a decade
  • Promise: honest reports, never inflated marks

The MeritHall method

Every lesson moves through four steps, so ideas stay with children long after the test.

  • Explain: a short, clear introduction
  • Show: a demonstration or worked example
  • Practice: guided questions in small groups
  • Check: a quick quiz and tutor feedback
